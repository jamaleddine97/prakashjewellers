Brand Story & Legacy

A Legacy Cast in Gold

It began in 1964 - not just as a jewellery store, but as a promise. A promise of purity, precision, and pieces that would pass from one generation to the next. What started as a symbol of quality, craftsmanship, and tradition in the heart of Mysuru has grown into one of the city's most beloved jewellery houses shaped by decades of trust and an unwavering commitment to the art of fine jewellery. Today, Prakash Jewellers stands as a living heirloom itself: a family name woven into the fabric of Mysuru's culture.
